## Scope reminders
Themes are not automatically separate implementations. A shared suspicion engine must not erase genuine rule differences. Tactics roster expansion, live microphone and continuous action games require separate designs and milestones.

## Future ideas not authorized
Physical scoring companion, custom-currency card tables, racing and platform fighting remain backlog candidates, not first-prompt requirements. Add only after an approved concept document.
